## Search Queries Used (in order)

1. `Crown Castle apartment Эрдэнэт Crown Castle Mongolia` — generic search, too broad
2. `"Crown Castle" Эрдэнэт барилга төсөл apartment` — better, found Facebook groups
3. `"Crown Castle" Эрдэнэт орон сууц төсөл АА бүс 2А хороолол` — found location details
4. `"Crown Castle" "Эрдэнэт" "2А" 45 48 51 2026 ашиглалтанд` — found unit sizes
5. `CROWN_CASTLE_LUXURY_APARTMENT Б блок Эрдэнэт 88723237` — found pricing
6. `"Crown Castle" Эрдэнэт хотхон ухаалаг харуул хамгаалалт` — found features

## Notes

- Facebook web search results often contain OCR'd text from images — stitch together multiple posts for complete data
- Prices varied by block (A block premium vs B block)
- Spring promotion ("Хаврын онцгой урамшуулал") was active at time of research
- The project has ~6,600 Facebook page followers (as of June 2026)
