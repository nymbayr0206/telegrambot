# Book / PDF to Social Media Series

Use this reference when the user uploads a book, report, or long PDF and wants social media posts from the most important facts, lessons, or ideas.

## Workflow

1. Extract metadata, page count, table of contents, and representative sample pages.
2. Identify the content type:
   - psychology/self-development
   - business/leadership
   - technical/educational
   - memoir/biography
   - report/market research
3. Create a copyright-safe content map:
   - summarize ideas in original wording
   - avoid long verbatim excerpts
   - use only short quotes when necessary and attribute them
   - frame content as education/reflection, not diagnosis/legal/medical advice unless qualified
4. Recommend a series size:
   - **10-post pilot** for testing engagement and tone
   - **24–30 posts** for a full month series from a book-length work
   - larger evergreen banks only after the pilot works
5. For each post, provide:
   - hook
   - key idea
   - caption
   - carousel slide outline
   - short video/Reels script
   - image/video prompt
   - CTA
   - hashtags
   - source chapter/page notes for internal reference only
6. Store drafts with metadata and send Telegram previews for approval before publishing.

## Suggested series structure for book-length content

- Posts 1–3: awareness hooks and definitions
- Posts 4–10: core concepts / symptoms / principles
- Posts 11–15: relationship or workplace implications
- Posts 16–20: family, system, or background causes
- Posts 21–25: self-check and personal development angles
- Posts 26–30: practical steps, summaries, lead magnets, carousel recap

## Copyright and safety guardrails

- Do not copy large passages from copyrighted books into public posts.
- Prefer paraphrase + commentary + practical examples.
- Attribute the book/author when referencing the source explicitly.
- For psychology/health topics, avoid presenting content as a clinical diagnosis. Use language such as “self-reflection,” “relationship education,” or “patterns to notice.”
- Avoid shaming groups of people; keep tone balanced and constructive.

## Example recommendation pattern

If a user asks “how many posts can we make from this book?”, answer with a practical recommendation:

- **10 posts** as a pilot series to validate engagement.
- **24–30 posts** as the best full series length for a book.
- Explain that each core idea can become multiple formats: Facebook post, Instagram carousel, Reels/TikTok script, LinkedIn post, and newsletter.
