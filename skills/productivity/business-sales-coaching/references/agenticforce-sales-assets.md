## Weekly AI Automation Showcase funnel

Use a weekly free event as the business-presentation meeting for AgenticForce.

Purpose:

- educate the market;
- show real workflows, not theory;
- collect leads;
- introduce AI automation specialists / workflow architects;
- convert attendees to free diagnosis, then paid pilots.

Suggested 60-minute format:

1. 0–5 min — opening and promise: practical demos, no theory.
2. 5–15 min — common business problems: lost leads, manual copy-paste, slow reports, CRM not updated.
3. 15–35 min — live demos: Lead Follow-up Agent, Daily Report Agent, Customer Service Agent.
4. 35–45 min — industry examples, rotate weekly: retail, mining, healthcare, education, construction, HR, finance, logistics.
5. 45–55 min — specialist panel: AI Workflow Specialist, Automation Engineer, CRM/Odoo Specialist, Data & Reporting Specialist.
6. 55–60 min — CTA: book a free 20-minute AI Workflow Diagnosis; offer a 7-day paid pilot.

Weekly rhythm:

- Monday: choose topic + invite list.
- Tuesday: send invitations.
- Wednesday: follow up and confirm attendees.
- Thursday: run showcase.
- Friday: diagnosis calls + proposals.
- Weekend: clips/content + next week prep.

Targets per event:

- invite 100 companies;
- 30 direct messages;
- 20 follow-ups;
- 10 registrations;
- 5 confirmed attendees;
- 3–5 diagnosis calls;
- 1–2 pilot proposals.

## 25 customer discovery / closing questions

Use these to diagnose before pitching AI.

1. How do you currently receive new leads or customer inquiries?
2. Where do those leads go after they come in?
3. Who is responsible for following up with each lead?
4. How fast does your team usually respond to a new inquiry?
5. How many leads do you think are missed or forgotten every week?
6. What repetitive admin work takes the most time for your team?
7. Which process still depends too much on manual copy-paste?
8. What reports do managers need every day or every week?
9. How long does it take to prepare those reports now?
10. What happens when a salesperson or staff member forgets to follow up?
11. If one hot lead is not followed up, how much potential revenue could be lost?
12. How many hours per week does your team spend on manual follow-up, reporting, or data entry?
13. If we saved your team 5–10 hours per week, where would that time be better used?
14. What is more expensive for you right now: missed sales, slow response, or staff time?
15. Which part of your operation creates the most stress or confusion?
16. If you could automate only one workflow first, what would it be?
17. Which department needs AI automation most urgently: sales, customer service, HR, operations, finance, or management reporting?
18. Who else in your company should be involved in deciding this?
19. What system must this connect with: Google Sheets, Odoo, Gmail, Facebook, website, Telegram, or another CRM?
20. What would a successful 7-day pilot look like for you?
21. If we can automate this workflow in 7 days, would you be open to testing a pilot?
22. Would it make sense to start small with one workflow instead of a big project?
23. If the pilot proves value, would you consider monthly automation support?
24. Is there any reason we should not schedule the workflow diagnosis this week?
25. Who are 2–3 other business owners you know who also struggle with lead follow-up, reporting, or manual operations?

Best 15-minute discovery set:

1. How do leads/customers come in today?
2. Where are they recorded?
3. Who follows up and how fast?
4. What repetitive work wastes the most time?
5. What happens when follow-up is missed?
6. If we automate one workflow first, which one matters most?
7. If we can prove it in 7 days, are you open to a small pilot?
