### Key Articles
- **Art. 3** — Company forms (LLC / ХК)
- **Art. 5** — LLC rules (max 50 shareholders)
- **Art. 11** — Establishment (register within 30 days)
- **Art. 12** — Founder eligibility
- **Art. 15** — Registration deadline (30 days)
- **Art. 16** — Charter (Дүрэм) contents
- **Art. 17** — Registration review (2 business days)
- **Art. 30** — Charter capital (no minimum for domestic)
- **Art. 32** — Share classes (common/preferred)
- **Art. 75** — Board of Directors (optional for LLC, mandatory 9+ for public)
- **Art. 83** — Executive director (mandatory, cannot be board chair)
- **Art. 84-86** — Fiduciary duties (2022 amendments)

## Law on Investment

- Enacted 2013, amended; new version adopted 2025
- US$100K minimum per foreign shareholder at 25%+ threshold
- Stabilization certificates: 5-18 years depending on investment size
- Sector restrictions: Mining, banking, media, telecom for foreign SOEs at 33%+

## Tax Law (2026)

- CIT: 10% up to MNT 6B, 25% above
- VAT: 10%, mandatory at MNT 50M turnover
- Tax year: calendar year
- Annual return: Feb 15
- Penalties: 0.05%/day late filing

## Law on Registration of Legal Persons (2003)
- Governs registration procedures via burtgel.gov.mn
- UB-03 form, UB-12 form for document submissions

## Х тайлан (Zero-Activity Report) Rules
- Only valid when NO income earned AND NO expenses incurred — **binary**: activity or no activity
- ANY bank transaction = activity = Х тайлан is inapplicable (no minimum income threshold)
- Filing Х тайлан while having bank transactions = discrepancy detected via cross-reference (ТЕХ 34)
- If Х тайлан is approved, NGO can file annually instead of quarterly
