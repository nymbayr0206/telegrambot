# Digital Applied — 5-Stage Agentic AI Maturity Model

**Source:** https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/agentic-ai-maturity-model-enterprise-self-assessment-guide

**Key stat:** 88% of AI agent projects never reach production.

## 6 Assessment Dimensions (score 1-5)

1. **Infrastructure** — agent-specific tooling, observability, memory management
2. **Governance** — accountability, audit trails, risk tiering, compliance
3. **Data** — quality, access, RAG infrastructure, unified platform
4. **Talent** — agent design skills, AI literacy, distributed expertise
5. **Culture** — AI-human collaboration, psychological safety, resistance level
6. **Outcomes** — measurable business impact, ROI, competitive advantage

## 5 Maturity Stages

### Stage 1: Exploration (~40-50% of enterprises)
- Leadership awareness emerging, ad-hoc experiments
- **Action plan:** Educate C-suite, map use cases, identify AI champion, benchmark competitors
- **Success metrics:** Executive sponsor, 2-3 pilot candidates, $200K-$500K budget

### Stage 2: Experimentation
- Structured pilots, POCs, basic evaluation criteria, informal governance
- **Key challenge:** Preventing "perpetual pilot" trap
- **Action plan:** Define production criteria before building, build evaluation infra simultaneously, engage legal/compliance early

### Stage 3: Integration
- Production agents in specific workflows, CRM/ERP integration, formal governance
- **Action plan:** Build internal platform (tool registry, shared memory), formalize AI governance council, unify data access, measure business impact

### Stage 4: Orchestration (<10% of enterprises)
- Multi-agent collaboration, shared context, agent-to-agent handoffs
- **Action plan:** Design agent-to-agent handoff protocols, invest in advanced observability, distribute AI expertise across functions

### Stage 5: Autonomous Operations (<3% of enterprises)
- End-to-end autonomous processes, human oversight for exceptions only
- **Action plan:** Agent-native process design (don't retrofit), ecosystem integration, continuous capability expansion

## Common Maturity Blockers
1. **Perpetual pilot trap** — Fix: define production criteria upfront
2. **Data quality debt** — Fix: data quality audit before pilot begins
3. **Governance as veto** — Fix: include governance in design review, not just deployment approval
4. **Central AI team bottleneck** — Fix: invest in internal enablement program

## Scoring Interpretation
- Average 1.0-1.9 = Stage 1
- Average 2.0-2.9 = Stage 2
- Average 3.0-3.9 = Stage 3
- Average 4.0-4.9 = Stage 4
- Average 5.0 = Stage 5
- Any single dimension scoring 1 while average is 3+ → **critical bottleneck**
