## Core translation

Original playbook idea:

> Build through activity, follow-up, field training, leadership duplication, recognition, and daily accountability.

AgenticForce translation:

> Prospect daily → book discovery → diagnose one workflow → sell a 7-day pilot → deliver fast → convert to monthly support → ask for referrals → train others to repeat.

## AgenticForce primary offer

Start with **AI Lead Follow-up Agent**:

- captures website/Facebook/Zangia/manual leads
- saves to Google Sheets or Odoo
- sends Telegram notification
- scores hot/warm/cold
- drafts follow-up message/email
- reminds salesperson
- produces weekly report

Why this offer works:

- easy to understand
- easy to demo
- every business understands lost leads
- fits the user's existing Zangia + Google Sheets + Telegram + Odoo direction
- can expand into CRM, reporting, support, and content automation

## Sales process

1. Prospect companies with manual/repetitive work.
2. Send short outreach message to book a 15-minute discovery call.
3. Ask discovery questions about leads, CRM, reports, follow-up, and manual work.
4. Diagnose one main workflow.
5. Recommend one focused AI agent.
6. Offer a 7-day pilot instead of a large project.
7. Implement quickly and show value.
8. Convert to monthly automation/support.
9. Ask for 2–3 referrals.

## Weekly showcase strategy

The user proposed weekly no-cost automation showcases where companies are invited and specialists/engineers demonstrate workflows.

Recommended positioning:

- Event name: AI Automation Showcase Mongolia / AgenticForce AI Workflow Demo Day / Бизнесийн AI автоматжуулалтын өдөрлөг
- Specialists should be positioned as AI Automation Specialists, AI Workflow Architects, CRM Automation Specialists, AI Agent Implementation Specialists, or Business Process Automation Consultants.
- Daily activity is for prospecting and follow-up; weekly showcase is for authority, demos, lead capture, and conversion.

Recommended 60-minute event:

1. 0–5 min: opening and promise: real workflows, not theory
2. 5–15 min: business problems: lost leads, manual reports, CRM not updated
3. 15–35 min: live demos: lead follow-up agent, daily report agent, customer service agent
4. 35–45 min: industry examples
5. 45–55 min: specialist panel
6. 55–60 min: CTA to free AI Workflow Diagnosis and 7-day pilot

Weekly rhythm:

- Monday: choose topic + invite list
- Tuesday: send invitations
- Wednesday: follow up and confirm attendees
- Thursday: run showcase
- Friday: diagnosis calls and proposals
- Weekend: content clips + next week prep

## 25 customer questions for AI automation discovery and closing

Problem discovery:

1. How do you currently receive new leads or customer inquiries?
2. Where do those leads go after they come in?
3. Who is responsible for following up with each lead?
4. How fast does your team usually respond to a new inquiry?
5. How many leads do you think are missed or forgotten every week?
6. What repetitive admin work takes the most time for your team?
7. Which process still depends too much on manual copy-paste?
8. What reports do managers need every day or every week?
9. How long does it take to prepare those reports now?
10. What happens when a salesperson or staff member forgets to follow up?

Cost and impact:

11. If one hot lead is not followed up, how much potential revenue could be lost?
12. How many hours per week does your team spend on manual follow-up, reporting, or data entry?
13. If we saved your team 5–10 hours per week, where would that time be better used?
14. What is more expensive for you right now: missed sales, slow response, or staff time?
15. Which part of your operation creates the most stress or confusion?

Decision and priority:

16. If you could automate only one workflow first, what would it be?
17. Which department needs AI automation most urgently: sales, customer service, HR, operations, finance, or management reporting?
18. Who else in your company should be involved in deciding this?
19. What system must this connect with: Google Sheets, Odoo, Gmail, Facebook, website, Telegram, or another CRM?
20. What would a successful 7-day pilot look like for you?

Closing:

21. If we can automate this workflow in 7 days, would you be open to testing a pilot?
22. Would it make sense to start small with one workflow instead of a big project?
23. If the pilot proves value, would you consider monthly automation support?
24. Is there any reason we should not schedule the workflow diagnosis this week?
25. Who are 2–3 other business owners you know who also struggle with lead follow-up, reporting, or manual operations?

Best single closing question:

> If we can prove value with one workflow in 7 days, would you be open to a pilot?
