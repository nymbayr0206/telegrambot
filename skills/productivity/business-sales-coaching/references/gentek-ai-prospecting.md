# Gentek AI / AI Agent Automation — Buyer Profiles & Prospecting Framework

Structured buyer personas for selling AI automation agents (lead follow-up, social media, sales, CRM, customer service, reporting) to Mongolian businesses.

## Profile 1: "Бухимдсан Эзэн" — The Overwhelmed Business Owner

**Businesses**: Small shops, restaurants, salons, clinics (3-20 employees)
**Platforms**: Instagram, Facebook — manually managing DMs, orders, bookings
**Pain**: "I can't reply to everyone. I lose orders while I sleep. Hiring staff is too expensive."

| Dimension | Details |
|---|---|
| Monthly budget | ₮500K–1M/month |
| What they buy | 24/7 auto-reply agent for Messenger/Instagram DM that shows products and takes orders |
| Trigger event | Missed a big order, posted and couldn't keep up with comments |
| Objection | "I need to see it work first" → 7-day free pilot |

**Where to find them:**
- Facebook Marketplace / Instagram Shop — active sellers posting products daily
- Facebook Groups: "Бизнес, худалдаа, үйлчилгээ", "Монголын жижиг бизнес эрхлэгчид"
- Unegui.mn / Zar.mn / Marketplace.mn — shops with lots of listings
- Physical: Dragon Center, Nomin Department Store, 3-р хороолол жижиг дэлгүүрүүд

**Approach template (Mongolian DM):**
> "Сайн уу? Танд бизнесийнхээ сошиал, захиалга, хэрэглэгчтэй харилцааг автоматжуулах AI agent хэрэгтэй болов уу? Манай Gentek AI agent таны Facebook/Instagram DM, захиалга, борлуулалтыг 24/7 автоматжуулж өгнө. Өнөөдөр demo үзүүлээд, яг яаж ажилладагийг харуулъя. Сонирхол байна уу?"

## Profile 2: "Онлайн Худалдаачин" — The Social Seller

**Businesses**: Cosmetics, clothing, food delivery, service-based — heavy DM traffic
**Pain**: "I get 50-200 DMs daily, reply manually, lose leads at night and weekends"

| Dimension | Details |
|---|---|
| Monthly budget | ₮500K–1.5M/month |
| What they buy | Full social media sales agent — auto-reply, catalog show, order capture, FAQ |
| Trigger event | Customer complained about slow reply, or competitor started using automation |
| Objection | "Will it sound robotic?" → demo with natural Mongolian responses |

**Where to find them:**
- Instagram — shops with 500+ followers, frequent story posts, mention "DM for order"
- Facebook Pages — active commerce pages with many reviews
- Messenger — their own inbox shows how many unanswered messages they have

**Approach template (Mongolian DM, pain-first):**
> "Сайн уу? Танай Instagram-ыг харлаа, маш олон захиалга ирдэг юм байна. Хариулах agent ашиглаж байна уу? Манай Gentek AI agent DM-д 7/24 хариулж, бүтээгдэхүүн үзүүлж, захиалга авдаг. 7 хоног үнэгүй туршуулъя?"

## Profile 3: "Сэтгэл Зовсон Менежер" — The Stressed Office Manager

**Businesses**: Companies with 5-50 employees — real estate agencies, logistics firms, service companies, small offices
**Pain**: "Employees don't clock in on time, I spend days on reports, workflow is manual"

| Dimension | Details |
|---|---|
| Monthly budget | ₮1–3M/month (corporate budget) |
| What they buy | Automated scheduling, report generation, CRM updates, workflow agents |
| Trigger event | Missed a deadline, lost a client due to slow response, new regulation requiring reporting |
| Objection | "Our team won't adapt" → offer training + pilot on one workflow |

**Where to find them:**
- LinkedIn Mongolia — office managers, operations managers
- Referrals from existing clients
- Business directories: Зangia.mn, business directories
- Direct: walk into office buildings in CBD with a demo

**Approach template (Mongolian, problem-oriented):**
> "Сайн байна уу? Танай байгууллагын цагийн хуваарь, тайлан, CRM зэрэг давтамжтай ажлуудыг автоматжуулах AI agent танилцуулъя. 7 хоногт нэг процесс автоматжуулаад, үр дүнг нь харуулъя. Танилцуулга хийхэд 20 минут л хангалттай."

## Universal sales progression

1. **Start with pain**, not solution — "Таны алдсан захиалгыг миний agent аварна"
2. **Offer a FREE 7-day pilot** on ONE workflow (no risk, proves value)
3. **Once agent is running** — faster replies, more orders captured
4. **Convert to subscription** — ₮500K–1.5M/month, "санаа зоволтгүй цалин"
5. **Upsell more agents** — reporting agent, CRM agent, follow-up agent
6. **Ask for referrals** — "Танай салбарын хэн нэгэнд ч хэрэгтэй байж болох?"

3. **Marketplace.mn / Unegui.mn / Zar.mn** — Frequent sellers (they need order management)

5. **Referrals** — existing clients → their business network
