# St. Gallen Business Model Navigator — 60 Business Model Patterns

**Source:** Gassmann, Frankenberger, Csik — University of St. Gallen
**Extracted from:** https://businessmodelnavigator.com/explore (official site, June 2026)
**Relevance:** Business model innovation coaching, AgenticForce positioning, startup strategy, competitive differentiation

---

## What this is

The St. Gallen researchers analyzed 250+ companies and identified 55 (now 60) recurring business model patterns. Business model innovation = taking a proven pattern from one industry and applying it to yours. These patterns are the raw material for rethinking how you create, deliver, and capture value.

---

## The 60 Patterns

### 💰 Pricing Strategy Patterns

| # | Pattern | Core Idea | Examples |
|---|---------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Add-on** | Core offering is cheap; extras drive price up. Customer tailors to needs. | Ryanair, SAP, Sega, Salesforce, AWS |
| 4 | **Auction** | Highest bidder wins. Price discovered dynamically. | eBay, Google Ads, Lufthansa |
| 15 | **Flat Rate** | Single fixed fee regardless of usage. Simplifies pricing. | Netflix, Spotify, LinkedIn, SBB |
| 35 | **Pay Per Use** | Pay for what you consume. Flexibility for customer. | AWS, Google Cloud, Mobility, Flyeralarm |
| 36 | **Pay What You Want** | Buyer chooses price (even zero). Relies on social norms. | Humble Bundle, Panera Bread |
| 48 | **Subscription** | Regular recurring fee (monthly/yearly). Steady revenue. | Amazon Kindle, Hilti, SBB |

## How to use in coaching

1. **Diagnosis phase:** Ask "Which 2-3 patterns does your current business use?" The answer reveals implicit strategy.
2. **Ideation phase:** Pick 3 patterns from different categories. Ask "What if we applied this pattern to our business?"
3. **Competitive differentiation:** Find a pattern no competitor uses in your industry → blue ocean opportunity.
4. **AgenticForce positioning:** Which patterns fit AI automation services? (Subscription, Solution Provider, Pay Per Use, Performance-based Contracting, Whitelabel, etc.)
